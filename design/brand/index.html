Haggling, but make it sweet.

Sweetspot is where two prices melt into one deal. Our brand is warm, fair and quietly clever: a shop's AI agent that negotiates inside the merchant's rules and closes every deal with PayPal.

Two prices, one sweet spot

Every negotiation has a shopper's price and a shop's price. Sweetspot's job is to find the point where both are happy, and our identity is built entirely on that moment: two drops sliding together until they melt into one.

Coral, the shopper. Warm, hopeful, making an offer. Coral is the colour of the person on the other side.

Plum, the shop's agent. Considered and dependable, holding the merchant's line.

Berry, the deal. When they agree, coral and plum melt into berry. It's the only place our gradient ever appears.

Who we're talking to

Merchants, our customers
Independent online shops selling things people think twice about buying. They want sales back without public markdowns, and they need to trust that the agent never goes below their floor. Speak to them about control and money won back.
Shoppers, their customers
They meet Sweetspot inside a shop, in that shop's own look, with a small "Negotiated by Sweetspot" badge. They should leave feeling they were treated fairly, even when the agent held the line.

Voice

We sound like a good shopkeeper: friendly, straight-talking and fair. Never a pushy salesperson, never a trading terminal.

Warm
Friendly and human. We say "deal", not "transaction".
Fair
We explain the why when we say no, and never invent urgency.
Clear
Prices, limits and next steps in plain words. Numbers first.
Quietly clever
The AI is impressive; we let the result show it, not the jargon.

How the agent talks to shoppers

Do
  • "That's below what I can do on this grinder. I can do $232."
  • "Meet me at $219 and I'll ship it free."
  • "Deal. $214.00 with free shipping, held for you for 15 minutes."
Don't
  • "Only 2 left! Offer expires in 60 seconds!" (fake scarcity)
  • "Your offer has been rejected." (cold, no reason, no counter)
  • "As an AI language model, I'm unable to…" (breaks the experience)

How we talk to merchants

Do
  • "$3,480 won back this week from shoppers who were about to leave."
  • "Your agent never goes below your floor. Every offer is checked in code."
  • "Set a floor, a maximum discount and the extras you're happy to give."
Don't
  • "Leverage cutting-edge AI to supercharge conversions."
  • "Revolutionise your pricing strategy."
  • Claims about results we haven't measured.

Colour: Berry

A berry palette with one rule above all: the gradient belongs to the deal. Everything else is calm blush, deep Jam and clear ink, so the moment two prices melt together is the most colourful thing on screen.

Five rules that keep it ours

  1. One gradient, one momentCoral → berry → plum appears only in the deal drop and the logo. Never on backgrounds, buttons, cards or text.
  2. Jam, not blackDeep surfaces like this one, headline numbers and primary buttons use Jam, never near-black or navy.
  3. Plum, not electric violetThe shop agent is a red-leaning purple that reads as fruit. Generic AI violets are off the table.
  4. Leaf for money wonLeaf green marks revenue won back and paid deals, like $3,480 won back. Never decoration.
  5. Glaze, not glowDrops get a soft sugar-glaze highlight. No neon halos, glassmorphism or colour blurs.

Proportion

Blush 58%White 16%Ink 12%Jam 8%

Coral, berry and plum together stay under about 6% of a screen. Their rarity is what makes them land.

Motion

Motion tells the story of the negotiation: things travel toward agreement, bounce off limits, and melt when they meet. One authored moment per screen, never decoration for its own sake.

Toward agreement
Movement always means prices getting closer. Nothing drifts or loops without meaning.
Soft, like sugar
Springy, slightly elastic easing. Drops squish and settle; nothing snaps hard except a locked deal.
Locked means locked
An agreed deal gets one clear, final moment (melt, ripple, eye smiles), then stillness. Calm signals it's done.

The living logo

The Agent Tag's eye is the agent's presence, in the product and the brand. Try its states.

Idle blinks every 4–6 s. While waiting for an offer it glances at the shopper; while working out a counter it tilts gently (thinking); on a deal the eye closes into a smile and the tag gives one small hop.

Accessibility

Fair means fair for everyone. These are requirements, not nice-to-haves.

Contrast
Body text at least 4.5:1 and drops and UI shapes at least 3:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA). Coral never carries white text; berry and Jam do.
Never colour alone
Shopper and shop are always labelled; status chips pair colour with a word; the floor is drawn and named.
Chat for screen readers
The message list is a log live region that announces each new message once. The typing indicator is announced politely, not repeatedly.
Keyboard first
Offer input, send, accept and PayPal are reachable in order, with visible berry focus rings.
Reduced motion
All movement has a still equivalent; nothing essential depends on animation.
Plain language
Errors say what happened and how to fix it: "Maximum discount can't be more than 30%. Lower it, or raise the floor."

Research behind the choices

What shaped these guidelines. Most sources are secondary write-ups or vendor material, so treat them as direction, not proof.

  • Colour fit matters more than universal meanings; saturation reads as excitement, depth as competence. Labrecque & Milne, JAMS 2012; science vs. marketer beliefs
  • Purple or indigo in only 2 of 42 finance design systems sampled (directional). Lazyweb, 2026
  • Red prices lift perceived savings only on deep discounts and lower perceived value on small ones. Frontiers in Psychology, 2022; Drexel / Journal of Retailing
  • PayPal button colours and styling options. PayPal buttons style guide
  • Agentic UX: explicit confirmation for payments, a concise rationale for actions, clear records of what happened. Smashing Magazine, 2026
  • Faster responses and a clear explanation of a rejection raise satisfaction in price bidding. Harvard Program on Negotiation
  • Suppliers negotiating with an AI agent: many preferred it, and liked making counteroffers (vendor-reported). PYMNTS on Walmart and Pactum
  • Haggle chatbots in e-commerce issue a one-off code that expires within hours (Nibble at Industry West). Digital Commerce 360
  • Accessible chat: log role, announce new messages once, debounce streaming. Toyota design system; streaming live regions
